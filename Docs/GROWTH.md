## P2-W provider credit integration (GRW-P2-W; ADR-048 Proposed, KX-30 section 14; migration `20261217100000_growth_provider_credit_v2`)

Provider credit is the Clinic's CR-05 wallet (one per organisation). It is **a separate system from patient credit** (Kiorex `growth/rewards`): no shared table, no foreign key, no conversion in either direction, no provider-to-provider transfer, no cash-out, never patients' money. Flags: `clinic.provider_credit_v2` (default ON), `clinic.usage_bundles` (off until the market enables it). Code: `apps/api/src/credits/`.

- **Ledger**: `CreditLedgerEntry` gains `category` (`SERVICE_RECOVERY|ONBOARDING|PROMOTIONAL|REFERRAL|ADJUSTMENT`), `campaignRef`, `restrictions`, `idempotencyKey` (unique per wallet). A database trigger makes it append-only: only `remaining` (the first-expiring-first cache) may change, nothing is deleted except by the cascade of closing the wallet (W-5). New reasons: `KIOREX_FEE_OFFSET`, `SUBSCRIPTION_OFFSET`, `DISPUTE_WIN`, `ONBOARDING`, `CONNECT_BONUS`, `USAGE_PURCHASE`, `MARKETING_SPEND`, `SPONSORED_PROMOTION`, `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_REVERSAL`.
- **What a lot can pay for** (`credit-categories.ts`): returned fees and goodwill: everything; onboarding and referral: fees, subscription, usage; promotional: also patient discounts. Expired lots and lots whose category or `restrictions.spendableOn` excludes a use are never drawn.
- **Fee draw-down** (`credit-fee-drawdown.service.ts`, called by `KiorlyInvoicesService.issue`): credit pays first (first-expiring lot first), the invoice keeps the remainder, never negative and never more than the payable. The settlement's higher-of rule and `creditsApplied` are unchanged (ADR-007, ADR-008); the credit is split over what each use owes after netting (commission after its higher-of offset, subscription after a prepaid period), commission first. One CONSUME per use with `invoiceId` and key `invoice:<id>:credit:<use>`: a replay consumes nothing twice. OFF falls back to today's single ADJUSTMENT consume.
- **Returns as credit**: a billed charge that is reversed returns once (key `fee-return:<chargeId>`), reason `DISPUTE_WIN` (dispute approved), `GOODWILL` (platform waiver) or `KIOREX_LATE_REVERSAL`. An unbilled charge is voided with no credit (unchanged).
- **Grants** (`credit-grants.service.ts`): onboarding, connect bonus (fired on the first APPROVED decision in `KiorexConnectService.applyDecision`), amounts from the market `credit` block (default 0, so nothing is granted until the founder sets them). Provider referral rewards are the existing `ReferralRewardService` (now with idempotency keys). Manual grants by a Super Admin refuse the spend-side and engine reasons; every grant without an explicit ADJUSTMENT expires (W-4).
- **Promotion holds** (`credit-promotion-hold.service.ts`, the provider side of provider-sponsored discounts; the promotion engine, review and budget caps are GRW-P2-S in W7): `reserve` at booking (keeps credit aside, balance unchanged, refuses when the clinic's promotion-spendable credit minus open holds is too small), `debit` at completion (one CONSUME `SPONSORED_PROMOTION`; takes what is spendable and records a `shortfall` for the invoice line), `release` on cancel, `reverse` after a refund (credit returns once as `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_REVERSAL`; it never pays the patient). References are opaque (`promotionRef`, `holdRef`); no patient id exists in any wallet table and a reference that names a patient ledger object is refused (W-1, W-8). **Not exposed over HTTP yet**: the signed reserve and release commands are P2-S.
- **Usage bundles** (skeleton): `GET /credits/usage-bundles`, `POST /credits/usage-bundles/:key/purchase` with `Idempotency-Key`; one CONSUME and one unit grant in `UsageUnitLedger` (append-only, units are never currency). Catalogue prices are 0 placeholders (NEEDS_HUMAN).
- **Statements**: the monthly statement (JSON and the bilingual PDF) carries a Credit section (opening, grants by reason and category, returned fees, use by purpose, expiries, closing) built from the ledger, so it reconciles by construction; `GET /credits/statement/:period`.
- **Views** (owner, group admin, clinic admin, branch manager, finance only): `GET /credits/wallet` (balance, lots by expiry and category, what the credit can pay for, held amount, history). The old `GET /billing/wallet` is unchanged. Web: `/credit` (and a link from Admin, Billing): `apps/web/src/features/credits/` (`CreditWalletPage`, `CreditStatementPanel`, `CreditBanner`), strings AR and EN under `credits.*`.
- **Daily job** (`credit-reconcile.service.ts`): expiry sweep, Sigma ledger = balance repair, Sigma remaining = balance, holds never exceed the balance, unit ledger never negative; one `CreditReconcileRun` row per run.
- **Tests**: unit `credits/credit-drawdown.node.test.ts` (shared contracts vectors, two properties, W-1 to W-3 structural guards, statement reconciliation), integration `credits/cr05b-credit-fee-drawdown.integration.test.ts` (acceptance case: 999 credit and a billed commission of 400 pays 0; dispute win once under replay; connect bonus once under concurrency; holds; bundles; reconcile) and `credits/cr05c-credit-guards.integration.test.ts` (W-4 to W-8 against the trigger, cross-tenant and role matrix); web `credits.node.test.ts`.
- **Contracts needs for 1.12.1** (do not publish from here): make `clinic.usage_bundles` a launch-locked flag (gate H-2 and H-25) so "OFF by default" does not need the market switch; document the three Clinic-side credit read routes (`/credits/wallet`, `/credits/statement/:period`, `/credits/usage-bundles`); optional `ProviderCreditGranted` and `ProviderCreditConsumed` payload fields `reason` values for the nine new reasons (the event schemas take any `[A-Z_]` reason, so nothing breaks).
- **Honest gaps**: (1) the outbox events `ProviderCreditGranted`, `ProviderCreditConsumed`, `ProviderCreditExpired` and `SponsoredDiscount*` are NOT emitted yet (the wallet writes audit rows; emitting needs the P2-S consumer on the Kiorex side); (2) the staff app card `kiorly/app/(tabs)/(more)/credit.tsx` is not built (needs the W5 owner's tile-registry edit); (3) the platform admin grant dialog still sends GOODWILL only (the API accepts the wider reason list); (4) cash-out, transfer and prepaid-pack pricing are deliberately not built; VAT treatment of credit purchases is counsel and accounting (H-25); (5) counsel on forfeiture of promotional credit when a tenant closes (H-24) is open: the cascade deletes the rows and nothing else is promised in the UI.

## Two credit systems and sponsored promotions (ADR-048; Clinic side summary, wave 7 close)

The Clinic holds **provider credit** only: the CR-05 wallet (one per tenant), the append-only credit ledger with categories and spendable-on rules, idempotency keys, fee draw-down (credit first, then the invoice), dispute-win and waiver returns as credit, the daily reconcile report (section "P2-W provider credit integration"). It never holds, reads or converts **patient credit** (a Kiorex ledger); there is no shared balance row and no cross-ledger entry. A provider-sponsored promotion is a provider-owned asset: configured and funded here (budget, reservation, debit at completion, release and reversal on cancel and refund), shown at Kiorex checkout as a sponsored line from a signed projection; the Kiorex fee is computed on the fee before the sponsored discount (`GROSS_BEFORE_SPONSORED`, policy `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_POLICY`) and a promotion never changes chargeability or attribution. Counsel and accounting gates: launch guide 11.24 to 11.31. The promotion package (P2-S) is wave 7 lane A2; this paragraph describes the rule, its as-built record states what is wired.

## Provider-sponsored promotions (GRW-P2-S; ADR-048 Proposed, KX-30 section 14.4; migration `20270107120000_growth_sponsored_promotions`; wave 7 lane A2, 25 Nov 2026)

**A sponsored promotion is a discount the clinic funds from its own provider credit, not a conversion of balances.** It is a provider-owned asset (ADR-041): configured here, budget and wallet here; Kiorex reviews wording and scope and quotes from a signed projection. Gated by `clinic.provider_credit_v2` (the wallet use "promotions") on this side and by the launch-locked `growth.sponsored_promotions` on the Kiorex side. No table references a patient credit object and none holds a patient identifier (`patientRef` is an opaque hash from Kiorex; a test lists the columns).
- **Model** (`apps/api/src/promotions/`): `SponsoredPromotion` (status DRAFT, SUBMITTED, IN_REVIEW, APPROVED, ACTIVE, PAUSED, EXHAUSTED, EXPIRED, REJECTED, ARCHIVED), immutable `SponsoredPromotionVersion` (trigger; an edit is a new version, a new review, and the running version stops being offered), `PromotionComplianceReview` (CONTENT, SCOPE, HIGH_VALUE; CHECK `decidedBy <> requestedBy`; a decided review never changes), append-only `PromotionBudgetEntry` and `PromotionAudit`, and `PromotionReservation` (the mutable state of one booking hold). Money is in minor units.
- **Budget** `reserved + debited - released - reversed <= cap`: the promotion row is locked `FOR UPDATE` for every write and a **deferred constraint trigger** refuses any commit that would break it (RC-S2); completion writes RELEASE (converted) and DEBIT together so a debit is never counted twice. 20 parallel reserves against a cap that fits 7 give exactly 7.
- **Rules.** Scope and eligibility accept only non-clinical keys (the clinic's own doctors and visit types, care type, new to the clinic, first Kiorex booking, minimum lead time, Kiorex only); the discount applies to the visit fee only, never above it; a percentage needs a per-booking maximum; one promotion per booking; the content guard (AR and EN, placeholder list until counsel, H-28) runs before the human review and blocks clinical wording, outcome promises and "free" next to clinical words; HIGH_VALUE (budget, per-booking amount or percentage above the thresholds of `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_POLICY`, read from the market `featureDefaults.sponsoredPromotions` until a config channel exists, `PROPOSED_DEFAULT`) adds a third review by a different approver.
- **Signed commands from Kiorex** (`marketplace/v1/promotions/{id}/`): `reserve` (200 `{reservationRef, expiresAt}` or 409 with `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `PAUSED`, `WALLET_INSUFFICIENT`, `LIMIT_REACHED`), `release` (always 200), `reverse` and `review-decision` (these two are **new, contracts 1.12.1 request**). The reservation lives as long as the slot hold plus the grace and a sweeper releases it. WALLET_ONLY needs wallet cover now (the hold is a budget hold; the wallet is untouched); WALLET_THEN_INVOICE allows a shortfall.
- **Booking** (`marketplace-booking.service.ts`): the optional `sponsoredDiscount` block is validated strictly (a malformed block is 400); after the booking commits the reservation is confirmed against the appointment (doctor, visit type, care type, lead time, newness, staff-phone self-deal `PF_SELF_DEAL`); **the booking is never refused or rolled back because of a promotion**: an invalid or stale reservation releases the hold and the answer says `sponsoredDiscount.applied: false` with the reason.
- **Completion** (`promotion-completion.consumer.ts`, a one-minute self-healing poller, not a hook in the check-in or completion code, so a promotion problem can never fail a clinical action): COMPLETED (or CHECKED_IN for 24 h) with a check-in time debits the clinic wallet exactly once (`SPONSORED_PROMOTION`, reservation, budget entries and wallet entry in one transaction); a debit without attendance evidence is refused (`PF_NO_ATTENDANCE`, hard); cancelled or no-show before completion releases; a refund or reverted completion after the debit writes REVERSE_DEBIT and returns the credit once (`SPONSORED_PROMOTION_REVERSAL`), never paying the patient. A shortfall under WALLET_THEN_INVOICE becomes a `PROMOTION_EXPENSE` invoice line on the next Kiorly invoice (credit never pays that line; the statement and PDF show "Promotion expense (sponsored discounts)").
- **Commission and revenue.** `KiorexCommissionDecision` and `COMMISSION_CHARGE_CREATED` carry `feeBasisMinor`, `sponsoredDiscountMinor`, `basisPolicyVersion` **only** for a sponsored booking (default basis `GROSS_BEFORE_SPONSORED`, so the flat fee is unchanged; the three fields are outside the decision hash); a booking without a promotion is exactly as before. A sponsored amount is provider-funded: neither Kiorex revenue nor a Kiorex expense.
- **Fraud (enforce list in policy, others shadow).** `PF_SELF_DEAL` (patient phone equals a staff phone, enforced), `PF_PRICE_INFLATION` (visit fee raised more than 5 percent over the reference within 30 days, blocks activation), `PF_NO_ATTENDANCE`, `PF_CLINICAL_INDUCEMENT` (enforced), `PF_FARM`, `PF_VELOCITY` (shadow; recorded in `PromotionAudit`); `PF_COLLUSION_LOOP` needs several clinics and runs on the Kiorex side.
- **Reconciliation** `PromotionReconcileService` (daily in the scheduler): RC-S1 per promotion `DEBIT - REVERSE_DEBIT = wallet CONSUME - wallet GRANT(reversal) + uncovered shortfall`, RC-S2 the cap, RC-S5 no patient-object references; a test injects a corruption and the check finds it. RC-S3 (every Clinic DEBIT has one Kiorex line in DEBITED) is covered by the Kiorex integration suite on each side separately; the daily signed comparison and RC-S4 (the D 500, S 100, C 150, K 250 example) need the Pay wiring (Kiorex gap 1).
- **Web** `apps/web/src/features/promotions/` (`/promotions`): three-step wizard, budget card with burn and reserved, debited, remaining, review chips, pause, resume, edit as a new version, archive, the append-only ledger; AR and EN, RTL, 320 to 1440 px, axe clean (Playwright `e2e/promotions.spec.ts`, 6 tests on a private stack).
- **Tests.** Unit 48 (budget math with 50 random legal sequences, eligibility vectors, content-guard corpus AR and EN, version validation, review outcome, every fraud rule one scenario) and 2 (invoice line); integration `promotions.integration.test.ts` 16 and `promotions-booking.integration.test.ts` 5 (review flow, immutability and append-only triggers, the deferred cap, the 20-parallel race, refusals with the contract reasons, wallet cover, booking intake through the real `createBooking`, completion debit once, cancel and no-show release, refund reversal once, shortfall, RC-S1, S2 and S5 with an injected corruption, commission basis fields, flag OFF); web node tests 9.
- **Gaps.** The staff app has no read-only status card; the Clinic reads `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_POLICY` from market `featureDefaults` (no push channel from the Kiorex config); `PromotionBudgetEntry.invoiceLineId` stays empty because the table is append-only (the invoice period is on the reservation and the audit trail); `PF_COLLUSION_LOOP` is Kiorex-side only; provider credit v2 stays the only clinic flag (a dedicated `clinic.sponsored_promotions` flag would need a contracts patch).
